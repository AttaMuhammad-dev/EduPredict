How the application fits together

From data to a useful view.

01 / Analyst

Upload a bounded CSV, select chart columns and train a demonstration model. Datasets and reports are scoped to their creator.

02 / Teacher

Publish a task to an assigned cohort, review submitted work and grade within the task's mark limit. Other teachers' cohorts stay private.

03 / Student

Create a student account, manage the profile and submit assigned work. Public registration cannot create a privileged account.

Synthetic only. The application's models use generated training data and a heuristic risk score. These demonstrations are not validated for real institutional decisions. Email is disabled in the public demo.
